# Maps and finding a colleague

Open **Field tools → Map & find me**. Tap **Start GPS**, then **Centre on me** after a fix arrives. The blue circle shows estimated accuracy; the green line is this foreground session's trail. GPS does not need an internet connection, but reception can be poor indoors, underground or under debris. Lantern displays the fix time so an old position is not mistaken for a current one.

**Online street map** is optional. Enabling it sends your IP address and viewed map region to OpenStreetMap's tile service. It does not automatically share your position with a colleague. No Google, Apple or Bing account or API key is required. This is a basic map, not turn-by-turn navigation.

Before losing internet, tap **Save nearby area offline**. After confirmation, Lantern sends the displayed GPS coordinate to the public Overpass service and saves roads, building outlines and amenity points within approximately 500 metres. This is a deliberately small basic vector map, not a complete city map. Way geometries can extend outside the queried radius. The saved area persists on the phone and works without internet; it replaces the previous area. Use **Delete saved map** to remove it. Map data and its saved-area location are private device data, not an encrypted vault. No addresses, routing, traffic, earthquake damage, safe routes or rescue availability are inferred.

Downloads are foreground-only, one at a time, at least one minute apart within the app process, limited to 30 seconds and 5 MB, with no automatic retry. Public services can fail or rate-limit requests. A failed download leaves the previous saved map intact. Check your downloaded area before an outage. Production deployment at scale needs a suitable data provider or self-hosted service rather than relying on public Overpass availability.

You can also **Import GeoJSON** from a legitimate map-data source. The file must be a WGS84 `FeatureCollection` containing Point, LineString or Polygon features, at most 5 MB, 5,000 features and 50,000 coordinates. Optional top-level `attribution` and `savedAt` strings are displayed. Longitude must be between -180 and 180, latitude within the Web Mercator range ±85.05112878. Unsupported geometries are rejected. Only simple outlines and points are rendered; polygon holes are outlines, not filled terrain. Data accuracy and licensing depend on the source. Provide an attribution string when preparing an imported map.

**Share my position** and **I need help — find me** each ask for confirmation and send a one-time coordinate, accuracy and fix time to the currently connected colleague. Review the message acknowledgement in Conversation. They do not call emergency services, reach arbitrary people, transmit through disconnected networks or guarantee rescue. Sharing a message is not live tracking; separate existing location sharing remains opt-in. The nearby connection is currently same-platform and foreground-only.

Map preparation is useful for hotel teams, event staff and field workers before connectivity is lost. In an earthquake or other emergency, Lantern is an additional local communication tool, not a certified emergency alert system.
